Stage 02 · Extraction

Two gates. A month → a handful of clips.

20 s sliding window visibility ≥ 60% frames · p ≥ 0.6 motion absolute ≥ 0.008 ffmpeg byte-range copy single-decode 219 s → 130 s

The bug we don't reintroduce: per-video normalized motion let a flickering IR lamp pass as movement. Fix = absolute changed-pixel fraction + timestamp masking.

The VLM taught the gate: 235B found 63% of "verified" clips had no octopus (mostly Right_Left reflections). → drop Right_Left; a VLM beats the detector as a presence filter.

Stage 05 · Segmentation

Pixel masks — smallest model that works.

Teacher · offline

GroundingDINO-tiny → SAM2

Video-propagation seeded from the most-confident frame. IR tool-bleed 11.8%→6.5%, colour bleed 15.5%→5.8%.

Student · deployed

LR-ASPP · 3.2 M params

Distilled from 4,412 auto-masks across 77 videos. Per-frame mask + area fraction.

Deployment win: trained with negatives → presence gate AUC 0.86, 0.99 vs reflections, 0% reflection false-positives. Beats the CLIP gate where it was weakest.

Honest limit: present-mask IoU plateaus ~0.47 (bar 0.85) — a video-diversity gap (62 train videos), not architecture. More distinct footage is the fix.
